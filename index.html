What the columns mean

Sorted by VOR — value over replacement, i.e. how many points this player beats the waiver-wire alternative at his position by. That's the number that matters, not raw projected points, because every team's quarterback scores a lot.

Grade is what round he's worth, scaled to your league size: S++/S+ are round 1, S is round 2, A+ rounds 3–4, on down to F for undraftable. Tier is different — it marks the cliffs within a position, so players inside one tier are near-interchangeable. Bye is his bye week. SoS rates his schedule 1 (easiest in the league) to 10 (hardest). Val is how far past his average draft position he's still sitting there; positive means he's falling to you.

Projections via Sleeper's public API (Rotowire), re-scored under your league's rules. Values marked * had no headline projection from Sleeper to anchor to, so they're a raw stat sum and less reliable. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

On strength of schedule: this API publishes no "fantasy points allowed by position" data, so difficulty is proxied by each defence's own projected fantasy output — units that collect sacks, interceptions and shutouts are the ones that smother offences. It's a reasonable proxy and useful for sorting matchups, but it is not the same thing as measuring what a defence gives up, so treat the numbers as a rough ordering rather than a precise forecast. The schedule and bye weeks themselves are exact, reconstructed from the week-by-week opponent listed on every projection.
